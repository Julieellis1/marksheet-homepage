Who it’s for

One platform. Six very different Mondays.

Marksheet is role-scoped, so each person sees only what they’re meant to see. Pick a role.

See every branch and every term without phoning the school.

  • Term-by-term view of results, coverage and fee status
  • Multi-Branch add-on: oversight across branches, with each branch’s records kept separate
  • Audit log of who viewed or changed sensitive records
  • Licence arranged personally, no surprise renewals

  • Branded report cards with your logo, signature and stamp
