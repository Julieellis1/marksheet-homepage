Pricing questions

Before you ask.

How much does Marksheet cost?

Licences start from ₦35,000 per school per month, or ₦100,000 per school per term. Larger schools use more AI credits and pay more than smaller schools. The exact figure is agreed with our team before activation.

What happens if our licence lapses?

Nothing is deleted. The platform soft-locks so you can still read historical records, and any renewal is agreed with our team before it takes effect. There is no automatic renewal.

How do we pay?

By invoice or Paystack, once your onboarding is confirmed.

Can we get a refund?

If you paid a registration fee and onboarding isn't completed, or you decide not to proceed before setup begins, the full amount is refunded within seven working days of your request. The Refund Policy has the full details.
