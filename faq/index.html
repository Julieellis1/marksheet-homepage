FAQ

Questions schools ask.

Exams & results

What if the internet drops during an exam?

Exam delivery and multiple-choice marking run on the school's local network with no internet at all. Answers are saved on the school's machine and sync to the cloud once the connection comes back.

How does result verification work?

Every published report card carries a unique code (for example MS-8KQ2X7L3P). Anyone holding it — a parent, a university, an employer — can type it on the public verification portal and see the authenticated summary: school, session, term and overall grade. No account and no phone call to the school office.

Does AI decide my students' essay scores?

No. Objective questions are marked automatically. For essays, Marksheet suggests a score against the rubric and the teacher confirms or changes it. The teacher's mark is the one that counts.

Can we use our own CA and exam weighting and grading scale?

Yes. The school sets how CA1, CA2 and the exam contribute to the subject total and which grading scale applies. Marksheet calculates totals, grades and class positions from those settings.

Getting started

Can we move records from our current system?

Yes. Download the CSV template for students or staff, fill it, and upload. Marksheet stages the file, validates every row and shows a named error report before anything touches live records. Only clean rows are committed.

Do we sign up and pay online?

No. Onboarding is sales-led: you book a demo, we learn how your school runs, and we activate your licence personally. Applying creates no payment obligation.

How long does the demo take?

About 25 minutes. We set up a session live, run a mock exam and publish a verified report card using your own class structure.

How quickly will someone respond?

Our team reviews applications and calls you within 24 hours on the contact details you provide.

Pricing & licence

How much does Marksheet cost?

Licences start from ₦35,000 per school per month, or ₦100,000 per school per term. Larger schools use more AI credits and pay more than smaller schools. The exact figure is agreed with our team before activation.

What happens if our licence lapses?

Nothing is deleted. The platform soft-locks so you can still read historical records, and any renewal is agreed with our team before it takes effect. There is no automatic renewal.

How do we pay?

By invoice or Paystack, once your onboarding is confirmed.

Can we get a refund?

If you paid a registration fee and onboarding isn't completed, or you decide not to proceed before setup begins, the full amount is refunded within seven working days of your request. The Refund Policy has the full details.

Data & security

How is student data protected?

Guardian consent capture, role-scoped access so every screen shows only what that person is meant to see, and a full audit log of who viewed or changed sensitive records. Each school's records are kept separate from every other school's.

Who owns our school's data?

Your school does. The school is the data controller for its own records and can export them as CSV, Word, PDF or Excel at any time.

Parents & app

Is there a mobile app?

Yes, an Android app. Parents, students and staff get push notifications — results released, announcements, homework — straight to the phone's lock screen, with no per-message cost to the school.

Can one parent see more than one child?

Yes. A parent login is linked to every ward they are responsible for.
