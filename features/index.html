The platform

Eighteen modules. One structured home for the term.

Start with the term you already run. Marksheet gives it a shape that can be taught, examined, computed, verified and audited without re-typing anything.

ASSESSMENT

Exams & CBT

Build papers from a shared bank, deliver online or completely offline on the school LAN, grade MCQs instantly and get rubric-grounded AI help for essays — then feed clean scores straight into results.

Read more

ASSESSMENT

Results & Report Cards

Weight CA1, CA2 and exams into subject totals, rank within class, roll up term averages and print broadsheets and report cards that carry a verification code any employer can check in seconds.

Read more

ASSESSMENT

Question Bank

One stimulus, many questions. MCQs and essays with model answers and rubrics, a clear draft → approved → archived workflow and a CSV import that validates before it touches live records.

Read more

ASSESSMENT

Homework

Teachers set MCQs and essays for a class, students take them at home, MCQs grade instantly, teachers mark essays once and parents see the published score for every ward without being asked.

Read more

TEACHING

Curriculum & Syllabus

The NERDC scheme is no longer a PDF on someone's phone. Upload syllabi once, see every topic by class and week, and track what was actually taught — with the same topic names the exam will use.

Read more

TEACHING

Lesson Notes

One template, one review flow, every note linked to its syllabus topic. Write by hand or draft with AI that follows the class-level guidance — then let the HOD approve and the next teacher inherit.

Read more

TEACHING Add-on

Period Tracker

Teachers tick off every syllabus topic they teach; the class captain verifies it. Two-way accountability replaces the ‘we covered it’ that nobody can prove at week 12.

Read more

OPERATIONS Add-on

Timetable

Lay out periods by hand, or let the solver build a collision-free timetable from staff availability, subject load, rooms and department rules. Lock what you like, regenerate the rest.

Read more

OPERATIONS Add-on

Attendance

Mark attendance on a register or scan QR ID cards. Toggle daily vs per-period mode, print cards from a template, and flow totals straight into the term report.

Read more

ADMINISTRATION

People & Promotion

Admission numbers that generate themselves, staff assignments that know who can do what, parent logins linked to guardians, and a promotion engine that moves whole classes without breaking history.

Read more

ADMINISTRATION

Class-Subjects

Link each subject to the classes that actually offer it — with science / art / commercial departments — so results, broadsheets and timetables all filter to the right students automatically.

Read more

ADMINISTRATION

Settings & Announcements

Branding, letterhead, grading scales, fee-gating and role-targeted announcements — one place to make the school look like itself and say what needs saying, with a full audit trail behind it.

Read more

ADMINISTRATION

Imports & Exports

Download a template, fill it, get a staging report with every error named. Only clean rows commit. And when you need a broadsheet or report card out, export it as CSV, DOC, PDF or XLSX in one click.

Read more

FINANCE

Bursary & Fees

Define fee items per term and class level, record payments by cash or transfer, let the system derive each student’s balance and auto-nudge guardians on schedule with an editable, variable-powered reminder.

Read more

FINANCE

Fee Status

Cleared / partial / not paid is computed from what the bursar actually recorded — fee items vs payments — and is the single gate for exam and result access, not a box someone ticks by hand.

Read more

COMMUNICATION

Messaging

Staff, students and parents talk in scoped 1:1 conversations. Admins and HODs can also bulk-message ‘all teachers’, ‘all JSS2 parents’ or ‘parents by fee status’ — each as a private thread, with template variables like the student’s name and the class.

Read more

COMMUNICATION

Mobile App & Push

The whole portal in an Android app plus push notifications that delivers result alerts and school notices to a parent’s lock screen — at zero per-message cost, replacing paid SMS/WhatsApp threads.

Read more

COMMUNICATION

Support Tickets

Every request gets a ticket, a priority and a thread. Schools talk to the platform team in the same place they manage everything else — nothing gets lost in WhatsApp.

Read more
