About Marksheet

We built the system broadsheet week needed.

Marksheet gives the whole academic cycle one structured home, from syllabus to verified report card. It’s built in Lagos around the Nigerian school calendar, not bent into a generic school app.

What we believe

  • Teachers stay in charge. AI drafts and suggests; teachers decide every mark that counts.
  • Records should outlive staff turnover. When a teacher leaves mid-term, the next one inherits the notes and the coverage.
  • A result should be checkable. A report card that can’t be verified is just paper.
  • Schools shouldn’t be held hostage. No auto-renewal, nothing deleted on lapse, export anytime.
Confirm with Marksheet: the founders’ names, photos and story (why Marksheet was started), the year founded, the registered company name and RC number, and any school partners who agree to be named. The live site gives none of these, and they’re the strongest trust signals for a proprietor about to hand over student records.
