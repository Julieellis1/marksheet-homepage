How it works

Twelve weeks, four moves.

Pick a week to see what Marksheet does for your staff that week. It follows the term you already teach and gives each week a record that can be audited.

Set upTeach & trackCA & examsResults

Weeks 1–2

Set up the session

  • Create the session and its three terms
  • Load classes and arms, and link each subject to the classes that offer it (science, art, commercial)
  • Import students and staff from a CSV template. Nothing touches live records until the staging report is clean
  • Link parent logins to their wards and set your grading scale and CA weighting

Modules used

  • Imports & Exports
  • Class-Subjects
  • People & Promotion
  • Settings & Announcements

Weeks 3–9

Teach and track coverage

  • Upload syllabi once and see every topic by class and week
  • Write lesson notes in one template, or AI-draft them against the topic, then HOD review
  • Teachers tick the topic taught; the class captain verifies it
  • Set homework, take attendance and message parents in private threads

Modules used

  • Curriculum & Syllabus
  • Lesson Notes
  • Period Tracker
  • Homework
  • Attendance

Weeks 10–11

Run CA and the main exam

  • Build papers from the shared, reviewed question bank
  • Draft → HOD review → approved → scheduled
  • Deliver online, or in the hall on the school LAN with no internet
  • MCQs mark instantly; essays get rubric-grounded AI suggestions the teacher confirms

Modules used

  • Question Bank
  • Exams & CBT
  • Fee Status

Week 12

Compute, publish, verify

  • Weighted CA + exam totals, grades and positions compute from the school’s settings
  • Print broadsheets and branded report cards with logo, signature and stamp
  • Publish with a verification code any parent or employer can check
  • Parents get a push notification the moment results are released

Modules used

  • Results & Report Cards
  • Mobile App & Push
  • Bursary & Fees
